How it works

Four steps, in this order. Most of the work happens in the first one.

  1. Take your measurements

    One conversation about budget, commute, schools and the things you won't compromise on. Written down, so nothing gets lost.

  2. Try homes on

    You tour a short list that matches what you told us. No filler showings to pad the afternoon.

  3. Make alterations

    Offer, inspection, repairs, price. We negotiate until the terms fit as well as the house does.

  4. Collect your keys

    We track every deadline through closing and stay on the phone until the keys are in your hand.
